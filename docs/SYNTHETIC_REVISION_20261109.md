# Independent Mid source and degradation: procedural pilot contract

This document describes an **opt-in research path**, not a new default or a validated improvement. The frozen pilot protocol is `artifacts/revision-20261109/protocol.json`; it predeclares the baseline commit, train/evaluation seed bases, fixed budget and controls. No owner listening or production promotion follows from this implementation. Results must be recorded separately after the declared runs finish.

## Why the experiment exists

The historical `rich_synth::generate_mid_multitrack` selects both a source and a bandwidth-loss recipe from one source seed. In family 11, the source's own low-pass cutoff is calculated from the same recipe's cutoff. Consequently, source spectrum and degradation are structurally coupled. That is a potential shortcut, not proof that any trained model exploited it. Other legacy `generate`, `generate_mid` and `generate_low` families also use their damage cutoff to shape a source. Historical Mid-v3 training source lineage includes a dirty checkout and is not fully authenticated; conclusions about that checkpoint require qualification.

## Implemented contract

`rich_synth::generate_mid_multitrack_independent(source_seed, damage_seed)` calls the historical source generator using only `source_seed`, retains the resulting waveform exactly, and substitutes `Damage::mid_range(damage_seed)` for the **actual** degradation. Recipe version 7 marks this variant. The original source-defined spectral cutoff still affects certain source families; it is now a source parameter, not the cutoff applied as experimental damage. It is recorded per training example as `source_shape_cutoff_hz`. Source seed and damage seed must be chosen as independent schedules in the experiment design; a deterministic RNG does not by itself prove statistical independence of arbitrary chosen schedules.

`independent_damage_seed(base, index)` derives a repeatable per-scene seed with SplitMix64 from the independent schedule base and index. `rich_mid::train_independent` exposes this path through `rich-mid-train --damage-seed`; it trains from a fresh initialization only and does not resume legacy optimizer state. It records source seed, generated damage seed, source cutoff, actual damage cutoff, schedule base, objective settings and actual model dimensions. Generic legacy provenance remains present in the receipt and is not authoritative for this model; the separately recorded actual architecture is 3098→32→32 encoder and 184→32→6 head, 106,342 parameters, native 48 kHz with 1024-point base STFT. Preserve the source and training receipt for exact replay.

`rich-mid-evaluate --damage-seed` uses the same source/damage separation on a separately chosen source bank and degradation schedule. It requires a fresh output directory and records per-scene missing-band target power, candidate complex error power, and degraded-input identity error power. An independent damage cutoff can fall below the old source-defined cutoff, so a family label by itself does not establish an empty missing band. The independent evaluator uses a small absolute target-power threshold to identify null cases; the raw powers remain available. These STFT statistics presently use the first waveform channel; compare channels, time-domain changes, silence and healthy inputs separately before a quality conclusion. Identity error and absolute power should be examined alongside relative metrics, particularly where the denominator is tiny. Existing mean/LSD and false-add summaries have limited interpretation and are not perceptual scores.

The unit test `independent_mid_damage_never_changes_the_source` compares both the stopped family and a full-band family under two damage seeds against the legacy source waveform, checks recipe versions and verifies an active-source degradation changes. This is an invariance check; it does not establish generalization to music, independence across every possible seed, correct output from a trained network, or a perceptual benefit. Historical generator, command defaults and shipped weights remain unchanged.

## Bounded first comparison

The fixed initial comparison trains one unchanged baseline and one independent-damage candidate at matching source seed, architecture, optimizer settings and 100 updates. Evaluate both on the **same**, separately seeded 12-scene independent bank with the same eight-step solver; retain identity, available DSP baselines, raw case-level results and failed controls. One initialization and approximately 25 source scenes at four updates per scene are a smoke-scale comparison; small differences cannot establish a robust advantage. Record finite output, known-band projection and zero-strength behavior separately. Do not select seeds, checkpoints, thresholds or solver by inspection of this evaluation bank. Follow-up work needs fresh multiple source and damage seeds, crossed repeated degradations of the same target, healthy/no-damage and null cases, longer contexts, multi-channel metrics and listening on permitted held-out material.

The unchanged legacy training bank remains a necessary comparator: the independent candidate changes the paired data distribution, not the model capacity or training budget. To distinguish distribution shift from beneficial learning, paired evaluation must include both the historical and independent-source/damage distributions. A better error on one bank may come with regressions on another. This experiment neither implements natural-music training nor establishes the realism of synthesized vocals, codec defects, or recording environments.

## Limits and continuation

Only the Mid multitrack generator has this opt-in interface. The high-band and low-band source/degradation coupling identified above remains in their existing paths; the present change must not be described as a project-wide correction. The original source distribution, synthesizers, loss, model, and inference assumptions have not been redesigned. In particular, artificially source-shaped frequency gaps remain and need explicit source/degradation factorial controls; source content and intentional style cannot be inferred from a fixed spectral target. File-level results are procedural, and no manufactured-damage score demonstrates recovery of the unknown clean original of an arbitrary recording.

Independent optimizer states use `rich-mid-crossed-state-v1`; the legacy resume loader rejects them. This prevents an independent experiment silently switching back to coupled generation. Model inference uses the existing Mid field format; training provenance distinguishes the curriculum.
