## Bounded training pilot and negative outcome

The predeclared protocol in `artifacts/revision-20261109/protocol.json` trains a fresh legacy Basis Mid model and a fresh independent-damage model at the same initialization/source seed, architecture, optimizer and 100 updates (about 25 source scenes, four updates each). Damage schedule base is independently selected. Both are evaluated once on twelve unseen source scenes with eight solver steps, first on independent damage and then on historical coupled damage. The new evaluation records identity and DSP harmonic-prior powers on independent cases. No target-driven seed/checkpoint/sample selection or hyperparameter adjustment followed these observations.

Pooled complex NMSE is total first-channel missing-band complex error power divided by total target power, weighting cases by their energy. It differs from a mean of per-case ratios; neither is a perceptual score. The full per-case results and both summaries are in the tracked numeric receipt.

| Evaluation damage | Legacy training | Independent training | Identity | Harmonic DSP prior |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Independent | 1.016421 | 0.987710 | **0.977320** | 0.979943 |
| Historical coupled | 0.996486 | 0.992014 | **0.977327** | Not collected on this legacy route |

Independent training improves pooled complex error relative to fresh legacy training on these cases, but **both lose to identity**. On independent damage it also loses to the fixed harmonic prior. Mean per-case NMSE likewise improves from 0.969651 to 0.951782 on independent damage and 0.833783 to 0.821275 on paired damage; pooling must not conceal identity's advantage. This is negative evidence for promoting these short-trained models, not a conclusion that independent degradation is unnecessary. One initialization, a small bank and limited manufactured defects do not establish generalization or training convergence.

The evaluator's maximum local STFT passband error is 0.44335 / 0.21162 on independent damage and 0.07297 / 0.05196 on historical damage (legacy / independent training). This unnormalized local-STFT metric is not the whole-track Fourier projection contract; it also reflects leakage and synthesis. Treat these as unresolved preservation diagnostics, not a passing full-band guarantee. Realistic codec/AI-vocal defects, healthy full-track invariance, multichannel metrics, dynamics-v3 retraining and listening remain open.
